Step 1 of 3
Let's set up your business.
Two minutes of setup, then TecXFRA configures itself around how your business actually runs.
Step 2 of 3
What kind of business is this?
TecXFRA relabels itself and pre-selects the right modules across all 7 layers, Tec (Today, Expenses, CRM), X (Execution & Growth) and FRA (Follow-Ups, Revenue, Accountability), for your industry. You can fine-tune everything on the next step, and change this anytime in Settings.
Step 3 of 3
Turn on what you need, organised by the TecXFRA system.
Every module sits under one of the 7 letters, Tec (Today, Expenses, CRM) · X (Execution & Growth) · FRA (Follow-Ups, Revenue, Accountability). We've pre-checked what businesses like yours usually use, leave the rest on too, it costs nothing to have them ready for when you grow into them.
Step 4 of 4
Choose your plan.
TecXFRA rewards its first businesses: the very first 10 signups get their chosen plan free, permanently, 2 Enterprise, 3 Growth, 5 Starter spots. After that, every plan is a paid subscription, and Starter is priced to stay within reach of any business, anywhere.
You can change plans anytime after signup in Settings.
